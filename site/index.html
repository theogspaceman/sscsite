02 Company

About

Superior Solutions Consulting was founded to provide advisory and technical support services across the full contract lifecycle — from logistics and sourcing execution through business development, acquisition, and program support. We work with organizations that need experienced, execution-focused support without the overhead of a large consultancy.

Experience

Superior Solutions Consulting was founded from experience in international defense business development, program management, project execution, and consultative sales. Our background includes managing opportunities from pursuit through execution, coordinating with government and commercial customers, and aligning partners around complex requirements. We bring practical experience in proposals, contracts, logistics, budgeting, vendor coordination, and international operations. That foundation enables Superior Solutions to identify opportunities, build partnerships, and move programs efficiently toward execution.
